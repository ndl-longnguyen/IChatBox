Quick Integration Guide

To add the chatbox widget to your website, copy and paste the following HTML snippet right before the closing </body> tag of your webpages: 

<!-- IChatBox Live Support Widget -->
<script src="http://localhost:8002/static/ichatbox.js"></script>
<script>
    IChatBox({
        token: "{{ user.customer_key.key|default:'YOUR_LICENSE_KEY' }}",
        username: "Guest_" + Math.floor(Math.random() * 1000)
    });
</script>
